# Rua Lucas de Camargo - Vila Mendes

## Summary

This is the primary worksite for the emergency gabion wall reconstruction, roadway recomposition, and drainage system execution. It is located in the Subprefeitura/Vila Prudente. The emergency work was authorized due to the partial collapse of the gabion wall, causing roadway subsidence and compromising the drainage system, posing risks to pedestrians and vehicles. The project is managed by [[SECRETARIA MUNICIPAL DE INFRAESTRUTURA URBANA E OBRAS (SIURB)]] and executed by [[CONSTRUTORA KAMILOS LTDA]].
