# Viaduto/Ponte da Comunidade Húngara sobre a Marginal do Rio Tietê

## Summary

This is the primary worksite for the emergency structural recovery and recomposition project. It is mentioned multiple times as the 'Local' of the work in various official communications and the contract. The project aims to repair damages to its structural beams.
