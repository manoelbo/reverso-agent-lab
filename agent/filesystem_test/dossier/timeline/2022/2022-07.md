Parecer SIURB/ATAJ Nº 066317065 was issued by Paulo César Nannini, Chief Prosecutor of SIURB/ATAJ, analyzing the legal aspects of the emergency contracting for the work at Rua Lucas de Camargo. The parecer confirms the emergency situation under Art. 24, Inciso IV of Law nº 8.666/93 and recommends proceeding with the contracting, while also noting a delay in the process and recommending an investigation into functional responsibility.

Despacho Autorizatório de Abertura de Licitação 066317396 was issued by Marcos Monteiro, Secretary of SIURB, authorizing the emergency contracting of Construtora Kamilos Ltda. (CNPJ: 50.557.404/0001-59) for the work at Rua Lucas de Camargo – Vila Mendes. The authorization is based on Art. 24, Inciso IV and Art. 26 of Law nº 8.666/93, with a maximum execution period of 180 days from the 'Ordem de Início' (SEI nº 065723117).
