Paulo César Nannini, Municipal Prosecutor, issued a legal opinion concurring with the emergency contracting for the recovery of gabion walls at Córrego Morro Grande, based on the technical assessment of imminent risk and compliance with Law nº 8.666/93, Article 24, item IV.
