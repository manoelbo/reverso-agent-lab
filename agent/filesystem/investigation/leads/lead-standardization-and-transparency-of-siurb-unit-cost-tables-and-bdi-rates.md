# Context
Examine the application and transparency of SIURB unit cost tables and BDI rate negotiations across all emergency contracts. This includes understanding how base dates are chosen, how 'desoneration' is applied or waived, and the consistency of BDI rate requests and accepted discounts.

## Inquiry Plan

### 1. Formulate Allegations
- Allegation 1: Inconsistent application of SIURB unit cost tables across different emergency contracts, leading to potential discrepancies in project costs.
- Allegation 2: Lack of transparency in the selection of base dates for SIURB unit cost tables, potentially allowing for manipulation of contract values.
- Allegation 3: Inconsistent application or waiver of 'desoneration' in emergency contracts, leading to unfair advantages or disadvantages for contractors.
- Allegation 4: Discrepancies between BDI rate requests and accepted discounts across emergency contracts, suggesting a lack of standardized negotiation practices.

### 2. Define Search Strategy
- Identify all emergency contracts executed within a specified timeframe (e.g., the last 3-5 years).
- For each identified emergency contract, locate and analyze the associated SIURB unit cost tables, noting the base date used.
- Examine contract documents for clauses related to 'desoneration' and its application or waiver.
- Collect data on BDI rate requests submitted by contractors and the final BDI rates accepted in each emergency contract.
- Cross-reference findings with the 'deep-dive-2026-03-14T00-05-40.md' report for any relevant mentions or data points regarding SIURB or BDI.

### 3. Gather Findings
- Extract and categorize data on SIURB unit cost table base dates from all emergency contracts.
- Document instances of 'desoneration' application, waiver, or absence in emergency contracts.
- Compile a dataset of BDI rate requests and accepted BDI rates for each emergency contract.
- Identify any patterns or anomalies in the application of SIURB unit cost tables, base dates, 'desoneration', and BDI rates across the collected contracts.
- Interview relevant personnel involved in contract negotiation and approval processes to understand decision-making rationale (e.g., procurement officers, contract managers).

### 4. Map to Allegations
- Analyze the collected data to determine if there is evidence supporting Allegation 1 regarding inconsistent application of SIURB unit cost tables.
- Evaluate the transparency of base date selection based on contract documentation and interviews to address Allegation 2.
- Assess the consistency of 'desoneration' application or waiver to confirm or refute Allegation 3.
- Compare BDI rate requests and accepted discounts to determine if there are significant discrepancies supporting Allegation 4.
- Synthesize all findings to provide a comprehensive assessment of the standardization and transparency of SIURB unit cost tables and BDI rates in emergency contracts.
