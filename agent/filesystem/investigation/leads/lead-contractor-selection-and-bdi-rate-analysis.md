## Inquiry Plan

### 1. Formulate Allegations
- Allegation 1: The selection process for contractors in emergency projects may not consistently prioritize value for money, as evidenced by varying BDI discounts and final contract values.
- Allegation 2: Certain contractors, such as TUMI, may have been awarded contracts with minimal BDI discounts, potentially indicating preferential treatment or a lack of competitive pressure.
- Allegation 3: The criteria used to justify the selection of contractors and the acceptance of their BDI rates in emergency projects are unclear or inconsistently applied.

### 2. Define Search Strategy
- Identify all emergency projects involving ABCON, ATHRU, HHTEC, FP PROJETOS, TUMI, and JNB CONSTRUÇÕES.
- For each identified project, locate and extract data on: requested BDI rates, offered BDI discounts, and final contract values.
- Specifically investigate contracts awarded to TUMI to determine if a pattern of minimal BDI discounts exists.
- Search for internal communications, meeting minutes, or decision-making documents related to contractor selection and BDI rate negotiations for these projects.

### 3. Gather Findings
- Compile a comprehensive list of emergency projects and associated contractors, BDI rates, discounts, and contract values.
- Create a comparative analysis table for BDI rates, discounts, and final contract values across all identified contractors and projects.
- Document instances where BDI discounts were minimal or absent, particularly for TUMI.
- Summarize any documented justifications or rationales for contractor selection and BDI rate acceptance in the gathered documents.

### 4. Map to Allegations
- Compare the compiled data on BDI rates, discounts, and contract values against Allegation 1 to determine if inconsistencies in value for money are evident.
- Assess the findings regarding TUMI's contracts and BDI discounts against Allegation 2 to ascertain if there is evidence of preferential treatment.
- Evaluate the clarity and consistency of contractor selection and BDI rate justification processes based on gathered documents, mapping findings to Allegation 3.
