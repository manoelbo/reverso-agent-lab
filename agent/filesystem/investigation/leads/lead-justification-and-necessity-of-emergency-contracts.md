# Context
Investigate the justification for the 'emergency' designation across multiple high-value civil engineering contracts, particularly given the recurring nature of some issues (e.g., erosion, slope instability) and the delays between initial identification, authorization, and contract signing. This includes examining whether less urgent, competitive bidding processes could have been utilized.

## Inquiry Plan

### 1. Formulate Allegations
- Allegation 1: Emergency designations for civil engineering contracts were used in situations that did not meet the criteria for genuine emergencies, potentially circumventing competitive bidding processes.
- Allegation 2: Recurring issues (e.g., erosion, slope instability) were consistently handled via emergency contracts, suggesting a systemic failure to address root causes or plan for predictable maintenance.
- Allegation 3: Significant delays between the initial identification of a problem and the authorization/signing of an 'emergency' contract indicate that the situation was not time-sensitive enough to warrant an emergency designation.

### 2. Define Search Strategy
- Identify all civil engineering contracts designated as 'emergency' within the last 5 years.
- For each identified emergency contract, retrieve documentation related to the initial problem identification, assessment of urgency, authorization process, and contract signing date.
- Cross-reference emergency contracts with records of recurring issues (e.g., erosion, slope instability) to identify patterns.
- Obtain official guidelines and criteria for 'emergency' contract designations.
- Search for any internal audits or reports discussing the use of emergency contracts, specifically reviewing 'lab/agent/filesystem/reports/deep-dive-2026-03-14T00-05-40.md' for relevant insights.

### 3. Gather Findings
- Compile a list of all emergency civil engineering contracts, including contract value, contractor, date of problem identification, date of emergency designation, and contract signing date.
- Document the specific justification provided for each emergency designation.
- Identify instances where the same type of issue (e.g., erosion, slope instability) led to multiple emergency contracts.
- Calculate the time elapsed between problem identification and contract signing for each emergency contract.
- Compare the justifications and timelines against the established criteria for emergency designations.

### 4. Map to Allegations
- Map findings on the justification for emergency designations and adherence to criteria to Allegation 1.
- Map findings on recurring issues and their handling via emergency contracts to Allegation 2.
- Map findings on delays between problem identification and contract signing to Allegation 3.
- Identify specific contracts or patterns that strongly support or refute each allegation.
