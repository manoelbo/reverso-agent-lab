# Context
Investigate the recurring use of direct emergency contracting under Federal Law nº 14.133/21, Article 75, Item VIII (and previously Law 8.666/93, Article 24, Item IV) for multiple civil works projects, focusing on the justification for emergency status and the selection process for contractors in each instance, especially when BDI discounts are not offered or only one company responds.

## Inquiry Plan

### 1. Formulate Allegations
- Allegation 1: Emergency declarations for civil works projects are being used without sufficient justification, circumventing competitive bidding processes.
- Allegation 2: The selection process for contractors in direct emergency contracts lacks transparency and may favor specific companies, particularly when BDI (Benefícios e Despesas Indiretas - Indirect Benefits and Expenses) discounts are not offered or only one company responds.
- Allegation 3: The recurring nature of direct emergency contracting for civil works projects suggests a systemic issue rather than isolated, unforeseen emergencies.

### 2. Define Search Strategy
- Identify all civil works projects contracted under Federal Law nº 14.133/21, Article 75, Item VIII, and Law 8.666/93, Article 24, Item IV, within a defined timeframe.
- For each identified contract, obtain documentation related to the emergency declaration, including justifications, technical reports, and approvals.
- For each identified contract, obtain documentation related to the contractor selection process, including bids received (if any), negotiation records, and contract terms.
- Identify instances where BDI discounts were not offered or only one company responded to the emergency contracting process.
- Cross-reference contractors involved in these emergency contracts with their history of previous government contracts, especially other emergency contracts.

### 3. Gather Findings
- Analyze the justifications provided for each emergency declaration to assess their validity and adherence to legal requirements.
- Examine the contractor selection process for each emergency contract, noting the number of companies invited, bids submitted, and the rationale for the chosen contractor.
- Quantify the frequency of direct emergency contracting for civil works projects over the defined timeframe and compare it to other contracting methods.
- Document instances where BDI discounts were absent or only a single company participated in the emergency contracting process.
- Compile a list of contractors frequently awarded direct emergency contracts and analyze their relationships with contracting authorities.

### 4. Map to Allegations
- Map findings regarding the validity of emergency justifications to Allegation 1.
- Map findings on contractor selection transparency, BDI discounts, and single-bidder scenarios to Allegation 2.
- Map findings on the recurring nature and frequency of direct emergency contracts to Allegation 3.

# Conclusion

Scenario: Scenario 1 (Positive)

L'enquête révèle une utilisation récurrente de la contractualisation directe d'urgence pour des travaux de génie civil par la SIURB de la Prefeitura do Município de São Paulo. Bien que les justifications d'urgence soient documentées par des rapports techniques, la fréquence de ces événements et l'implication répétée de certaines entreprises, souvent dans le cadre de contrats plus larges de services techniques, suggèrent un problème systémique plutôt que des urgences isolées et imprévues. L'absence de détails sur le processus de sélection des contractants et les conditions de négociation (notamment les remises BDI) dans les documents fournis limite la capacité à évaluer la transparence et la concurrence, mais la nature des contrats cadres soulève des questions sur la justification de l'urgence pour chaque projet spécifique.
