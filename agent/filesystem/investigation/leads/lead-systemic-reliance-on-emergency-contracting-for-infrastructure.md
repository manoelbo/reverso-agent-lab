# Context
Investigate the root causes of the recurring and high-value emergency infrastructure contracts across multiple sites (EMEF, Aricanduva, Itaim Paulista, Jardim Novo Parelheiros, São Lucas, Rua José Martins Veiga), focusing on whether these incidents stem from inadequate proactive maintenance, insufficient urban planning, or exacerbated environmental factors.

## Inquiry Plan

### 1. Formulate Allegations
- Allegation 1: The recurring emergency infrastructure contracts are a direct consequence of inadequate proactive maintenance and insufficient investment in preventative measures.
- Allegation 2: Poor urban planning and zoning decisions contribute to the vulnerability of infrastructure in the identified locations, necessitating emergency interventions.
- Allegation 3: Exacerbated environmental factors, possibly linked to climate change or localized environmental degradation, are increasing the frequency and severity of infrastructure failures, leading to emergency contracts.
- Allegation 4: There is a potential for mismanagement or lack of oversight in the allocation and execution of emergency infrastructure contracts, leading to inflated costs or recurring issues.

### 2. Define Search Strategy
- Identify all emergency infrastructure contracts related to EMEF, Aricanduva, Itaim Paulista, Jardim Novo Parelheiros, São Lucas, and Rua José Martins Veiga over the past 5-10 years.
- Collect data on the nature of the emergencies (e.g., landslides, flooding, structural collapse) for each contract.
- Obtain records of routine maintenance schedules and expenditures for the infrastructure in the affected areas.
- Gather urban planning documents, zoning maps, and environmental impact assessments for the identified locations.
- Research meteorological data and environmental reports for the regions to assess changes in environmental factors.

### 3. Gather Findings
- Analyze the frequency and cost of emergency contracts per location and type of incident.
- Compare maintenance records with the occurrence of emergencies to identify potential correlations between under-maintenance and failures.
- Evaluate urban planning documents against the vulnerabilities observed in the emergency incidents.
- Assess the impact of environmental factors (e.g., rainfall patterns, soil erosion) on infrastructure stability in the affected areas.
- Examine the procurement processes and expenditure details for a selection of high-value emergency contracts.

### 4. Map to Allegations
- Map findings on maintenance records and emergency frequency to Allegation 1.
- Map findings on urban planning, zoning, and infrastructure vulnerabilities to Allegation 2.
- Map findings on environmental data and incident types to Allegation 3.
- Map findings on procurement processes and cost analysis of emergency contracts to Allegation 4.
