# Context
Analyze the financial commitment timelines for emergency contracts, specifically the delays between authorization, contract signing, and the issuance of financial instruments (e.g., Nota de Reserva, Nota de Empenho). Investigate whether work commenced prior to full financial formalization and the implications of such practices.

## Inquiry Plan

### 1. Formulate Allegations
- Allegation 1: Emergency contracts were initiated or work commenced before the full formalization of financial instruments (e.g., Nota de Reserva, Nota de Empenho).
- Allegation 2: Significant delays exist between the authorization of emergency contracts, their signing, and the issuance of corresponding financial instruments.
- Allegation 3: These timeline discrepancies led to irregular financial practices or increased financial risk for the contracting entity.

### 2. Define Search Strategy
- Identify all emergency contracts mentioned in 'lab/agent/filesystem/reports/deep-dive-2026-03-14T00-05-40.md'.
- For each identified emergency contract, extract the dates of: a) authorization, b) contract signing, c) issuance of Nota de Reserva, d) issuance of Nota de Empenho, and e) commencement of work (if available).
- Search for any mentions of 'Nota de Reserva' and 'Nota de Empenho' in relation to emergency contracts within the provided documents.
- Look for any discussions or reports regarding 'irregular financial practices' or 'financial risk' associated with emergency contracts.

### 3. Gather Findings
- Create a chronological timeline for each emergency contract detailing the dates of authorization, signing, and financial instrument issuance.
- Calculate the time difference between these key dates for each contract.
- Identify instances where the 'commencement of work' date precedes the 'contract signing' or 'financial instrument issuance' dates.
- Document any explicit statements or implicit indications of work beginning before financial formalization.

### 4. Map to Allegations
- Map instances of work commencing before financial formalization to Allegation 1.
- Map identified delays between authorization, signing, and financial instrument issuance to Allegation 2.
- Map any evidence of irregular financial practices or increased financial risk resulting from these timeline discrepancies to Allegation 3.
