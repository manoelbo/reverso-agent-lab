## Inquiry Plan

### 1. Formulate Allegations
- Consórcio SGP's technical analyses and recommendations under contract 049/SIURB/22 were insufficient or flawed, contributing to the recurrence of emergency civil works projects.
- The oversight mechanisms by SIURB for Consórcio SGP's work under contract 049/SIURB/22 were inadequate, failing to identify and address potential deficiencies in their technical analyses.
- The scope of work or deliverables defined in contract 049/SIURB/22 for Consórcio SGP did not sufficiently cover the necessary technical assessments to prevent recurring emergencies.

### 2. Define Search Strategy
- Identify and collect all available documentation related to contract 049/SIURB/22, including the contract itself, amendments, scope of work, and any performance reports.
- Gather all technical reports, analyses, and recommendations produced by Consórcio SGP for the five newly identified emergency civil works projects.
- Obtain internal communications, meeting minutes, and correspondence between SIURB and Consórcio SGP regarding contract 049/SIURB/22 and the specific emergency projects.
- Research the corporate history and past performance of Sondotécnica Engenharia de Solos S/A, Geribello Engenharia Ltda., and PlanServi Engenharia Ltda. on similar projects.

### 3. Gather Findings
- Analyze contract 049/SIURB/22 to understand the defined scope of services, deliverables, and quality control requirements for Consórcio SGP.
- Compare Consórcio SGP's technical analyses and recommendations for the five emergency projects against the actual outcomes and the nature of the emergencies.
- Examine SIURB's internal records for evidence of oversight activities, reviews of Consórcio SGP's work, and any concerns raised or addressed.
- Identify any discrepancies between the expected deliverables from Consórcio SGP and the actual documents provided, as well as any delays or cost overruns associated with their work.

### 4. Map to Allegations
- Assess whether the findings from the technical analyses and project outcomes support or refute the allegation that Consórcio SGP's work was insufficient or flawed.
- Determine if the documented oversight activities by SIURB were consistent with the contract requirements and industry best practices, to support or refute the allegation of inadequate oversight.
- Evaluate if the scope of work in contract 049/SIURB/22, as executed by Consórcio SGP, adequately addressed the technical needs to prevent recurring emergencies, to support or refute the allegation regarding the contract's scope.

# Conclusion

Scenario: Scenario 1 (Positive)

L'enquête révèle que le Consórcio SGP a été systématiquement impliqué dans l'analyse technique de plusieurs projets d'urgence, tous sous le Contrat 049/SIURB/22. Bien que les rapports du Consórcio SGP soient des analyses techniques, il n'y a pas de preuves directes que ces analyses étaient insuffisantes ou défectueuses, ou que la SIURB a manqué de surveillance. Cependant, le fait que le Consórcio SGP soit constamment sollicité pour des projets d'urgence suggère une possible lacune dans la portée initiale des évaluations préventives ou une récurrence de problèmes non résolus. Des recherches supplémentaires seraient nécessaires pour évaluer la qualité des analyses du Consórcio SGP et l'efficacité de la surveillance de la SIURB.
