## Conclusao incremental
The analysis now incorporates a sixth emergency civil works project, addressing the collapse of a retaining wall at EMEF Prof. Esmeralda Salles Pereira Ramos in Jaçanã/Tremembé. This critical situation, detailed in a technical analysis report (REL-008) by Consórcio SGP (under contract 049/SIURB/22), involves the collapse of a retaining wall next to the covered court, posing a risk to the school and nearby residences, and leading to an interdiction by Civil Defense. The report, with a reference month of October 2023, highlights the need for immediate intervention due to intensified displacement from recent rains and the approaching rainy season. The contract for this emergency work, valued at R$ 9,048,678.34 with a one-year execution period, was awarded to JNB CONSTRUÇÕES E SERVIÇOS LTDA, with a contract signature date of December 13, 2023. Direct contracting was used, with SIURB/OBRAS requesting proposals based on the SIURB unit cost table (July/2023 without desoneração) and seeking discounts on BDI rates. The requested BDI rates (e.g., 20.11% for Edificações, 22.76% for Pavimentação) are consistent with previous projects. The report emphasizes the need for an executive project to define a solution and continued monitoring. This new information further reinforces the pattern of direct emergency contracting for critical infrastructure failures, introduces a new contractor (JNB CONSTRUÇÕES E SERVIÇOS LTDA), and continues the involvement of Consórcio SGP for technical analysis under the same overarching contract (049/SIURB/22). The consistent request for BDI discounts and the use of varying SIURB unit cost table base dates are also reinforced.

## Hypotheses
- The emergency nature of the work consistently leads to direct contracting, now under Federal Law nº 14.133/21, Article 75, Item VIII (formerly Law 8.666/93, Article 24, Item IV), across different infrastructure projects.
- The BDI rate reduction offered by companies is a critical factor in their selection for emergency infrastructure contracts, consistently requested by SIURB/OBRAS. However, the case of TUMI CONSTRUÇÕES E EMPREENDIMENTOS LTDA accepting the requested BDI rates without a discount suggests that other factors, such as immediate availability or lack of other competitive bids, might also play a role in contractor selection. The consistent request for BDI discounts across all new emergency projects reinforces this hypothesis.
- SIURB/OBRAS maintains a standardized approach for requesting BDI rates and unit cost tables (SIURB Jan/2022, Jul/2022, Jan/2023, and Jul/2023 without desoneração) for emergency civil works, indicating a consistent procurement methodology, though the base month for the unit cost table varies.
- The Prefeitura do Município de São Paulo utilizes different procurement methods (e.g., emergency direct contracting vs. electronic auction for price registration) based on the nature and urgency of the goods/services required.
- Standardized quality control and regulatory compliance are critical aspects of recurring supply contracts, as evidenced by the detailed specifications for coffee.
- The involvement of consulting consortia like Consórcio SGP in technical analysis for emergency works suggests a multi-layered approach to project assessment and execution within SIURB, with the same consortium (Consórcio SGP) being involved in multiple emergency assessments under the same overarching contract (049/SIURB/2022).
