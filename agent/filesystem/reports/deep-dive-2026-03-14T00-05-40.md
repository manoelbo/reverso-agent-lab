## Conclusao incremental
The analysis now includes a sixth emergency civil engineering contract, this time for the containment of erosion on the banks of the Aricanduva River at Avenida Aricanduva nº 5.200. This contract, like those for Rua José Ferreira Paz, EMEF Prof. Esmeralda Salles Pereira Ramos, and Rua Desembargador Arnaldo Teixeira Mendes, is managed under the broader Contract 049/SIURB/22 with Consórcio SGP for specialized consulting services. The technical analysis, conducted by Consórcio SGP, identified severe risks due to the rupture of a gabion wall, potentially compromising a bridge structure. The work involves emergency intervention to stabilize the area, with an estimated execution period of 12 months. Similar to previous emergency contracts, it references SIURB unit cost tables (Jan/2023 base) and involves BDI rate negotiations. The specific contractor for this work is TUMI CONSTRUÇÕES E EMPREENDIMENTOS LTDA, and the contract value is R$ 9,048,678.34. The cost estimate was initially pending further surveys and project elaboration, but the contract value is now defined. This new emergency contract reinforces the pattern of high-value emergency works, often involving external consultants for initial assessments and direct contracting due to urgency. Notably, ABCON CONSULTORIA E ENGENHARIA EIRELI, a contractor from an earlier emergency, was also consulted for this project and offered slightly lower BDI rates than requested, though TUMI CONSTRUÇÕES E EMPREENDIMENTOS LTDA was ultimately selected. The coffee procurement remains a distinct, non-emergency process for goods.

## Hypotheses
- The rapid succession of events (inspection, contact with companies, authorization) in the emergency cases suggests genuine emergencies, as stated in the documents.
- The rejection by two out of three contacted companies in the first emergency case (ABCON) might indicate the challenging nature of the emergency work or unfavorable terms, making ABCON's acceptance significant. In contrast, all three companies submitted proposals in the second emergency case (ATHRU), suggesting more favorable terms or a larger, more attractive project. The third (HHTEC), fourth (EMEF Esmeralda), fifth (Rua Desembargador Arnaldo Teixeira Mendes), and sixth (Avenida Aricanduva) emergencies explicitly mention or imply consulting multiple companies and requesting BDI discounts, suggesting a competitive element even in emergency direct contracting.
- The timing of the financial commitment in both emergency cases (December 20, 2021, for ABCON after October 7, 2021 authorization; December 2022/April 2023 for ATHRU after October 7, 2022 authorization) significantly after the authorization to start work could indicate a delay in formalizing the financial aspects, or that work was expected to commence prior to full financial commitment. The HHTEC contract shows a similar delay between the Order of Service (May 2022) and contract signature (July 2023), and the EMEF Esmeralda contract also shows a delay between the Order of Service (May 2022) and contract signature (December 2023). The Rua Desembargador Arnaldo Teixeira Mendes case has a defined contract value and a 1-year execution period, but the specific contract signing date is not yet clear, only the report date (December 27, 2023) and the approval of the technical analysis (October 20, 2023). The Avenida Aricanduva contract was signed on December 5, 2023, after authorization on August 23, 2023, showing a similar pattern of delay between authorization and contract signing.
- The consistent use of SIURB unit cost tables and BDI rate negotiations across different emergency contracts suggests a standardized, albeit expedited, procurement process for such situations.
- The significantly higher contract values for ATHRU Engenharia LTDA, HHTEC COMÉRCIO E SERVIÇOS LTDA, FP PROJETOS E EMPREENDIMENTOS LTDA, and TUMI CONSTRUÇÕES E EMPREENDIMENTOS LTDA compared to ABCON Consultoria e Engenharia EIRELI (for its first contract) could indicate a larger scale of the emergency or more complex engineering solutions required for these projects. The EMEF Esmeralda case, while not yet having a defined value, also points to a significant intervention.
- The coffee procurement, being a 'Registro de Preços', indicates a planned, non-emergency procurement process for recurring goods, contrasting with the emergency civil engineering works. This suggests a diversified procurement strategy by the City of São Paulo.
- The detailed specifications and regulatory adherence for the coffee supply suggest a focus on quality control and compliance for consumable goods, which is different from the engineering services where the focus is on immediate risk mitigation.
- The involvement of Consórcio SGP in the technical analysis for the Rua José Ferreira Paz, EMEF Prof. Esmeralda Salles Pereira Ramos, Rua Desembargador Arnaldo Teixeira Mendes, and Avenida Aricanduva projects suggests that the City of São Paulo is consistently outsourcing initial technical assessments for complex emergency works to external consultants, prior to contracting a construction company for execution. This indicates a structured approach to managing complex emergency situations.
- The repeated use of 'Direct Contracting by Waiver of Bidding in Emergency Regime' (Dispensa de Licitação em Regime Emergencial) under Law 14.133/21, Article 75, Item VIII (which replaced Law 8.666/93, Article 24, Item IV) for high-value civil works warrants closer examination to ensure proper justification and adherence to emergency procurement regulations.
- The selection of TUMI CONSTRUÇÕES E EMPREENDIMENTOS LTDA for the Avenida Aricanduva project, despite ABCON CONSULTORIA E ENGENHARIA EIRELI offering slightly lower BDI rates, suggests that factors beyond just BDI percentage, such as technical capacity or other proposal aspects, might influence the final contractor selection in these emergency direct contracting scenarios.

## Suggested lines pool
1. **Justification and Necessity of Emergency Contracts** — The pattern of multiple high-value emergency contracts, some with significant delays between authorization and contract signing, raises questions about the true urgency and necessity of direct contracting, especially under the new Law 14.133/21.
2. **Contractor Selection and BDI Rate Analysis in Direct Contracting** — The selection of TUMI CONSTRUÇÕES E EMPREENDIMENTOS LTDA despite ABCON CONSULTORIA E ENGENHARIA EIRELI offering slightly lower BDI rates for the Avenida Aricanduva project, and the general lack of detailed reasoning for contractor selection in other cases, warrants investigation into the fairness and objectivity of the direct contracting process.
3. **Role and Oversight of Consórcio SGP in Emergency Assessments** — Consórcio SGP is consistently involved in the technical analysis for multiple high-value emergency contracts, suggesting a central role in identifying and defining these projects. Their influence on the scope and justification of these emergencies needs to be scrutinized.
4. **Financial Management and Timeline Discrepancies in Emergency Contracts** — Recurring delays between authorization, contract signing, and financial commitments across multiple emergency contracts (ABCON, ATHRU, HHTEC, EMEF Esmeralda, Avenida Aricanduva) suggest potential issues in financial planning or a practice of commencing work without full formalization, which could have financial and legal implications.
5. **Standardization and Transparency of SIURB Unit Cost Tables and BDI Rates** — The consistent reference to SIURB unit cost tables with varying base dates (Jan/2021, Jul/2022, Jul/2023, Jan/2023) and the negotiation of BDI rates across multiple contracts warrant an investigation into the standardization and transparency of these cost components in emergency procurement.
